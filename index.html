01 — Trajectory

From engineering toward the foundations of physics.

2024 — 2025

Engineering

I began university in Industrial Engineering at ETSEIB–UPC. The year introduced me to applied mathematics, programming, CAD and engineering problem-solving.

It also clarified the kind of questions I wanted to pursue: less about applying physical principles to design systems, and more about understanding the principles themselves.

2025 — Present

Physics

I transferred to the BSc in Physics at the University of Barcelona, where my focus shifted toward fundamental physics and the mathematical tools used to describe it.

In my first year at UB I received Matrícula d’Honor in five subjects, including Differential Equations and Vector Calculus, Thermodynamics and Electromagnetism.

Now

Theory + mathematics

My current direction sits at the intersection of theoretical physics, mathematics and computation.

Alongside coursework, I use programming as a way to explore physical and mathematical problems that are difficult to approach analytically alone.
